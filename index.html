Help & frequently asked questionsCamera access, OCR, file support and offline use.

Why is the camera unavailable?
Allow camera permission and open Scannity over HTTPS. You can import photos instead.

How do I extract text?
Open a scanned document and choose Extract text (OCR). English recognition data is downloaded the first time.

Which files can I read?
PDF, DOCX, XLS/XLSX, CSV, PPTX, TXT, Markdown, RTF, HTML, JSON, XML, SQL and SQLite. Older binary DOC/PPT files need conversion first.

Does Scannity work offline?
The app shell and saved scans work offline after installation. Optional OCR, auto-crop and reader engines become available offline after they have been downloaded once.

Terms of useResponsible use and document handling.

Use Scannity only with documents, images and codes you are authorised to access. Check OCR output and converted files before relying on them. Generated QR codes and barcodes should be tested with the intended scanners before printing or distribution. Scannity is provided as-is; keep independent backups of important documents.

For best results, use HTTPS (Netlify provides it) and allow camera access when prompted.
